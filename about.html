SAFE IMAGE SAVE

A small image tool with a clear boundary.

Safe Image Save converts the image you explicitly choose from the right-click menu. The conversion happens inside your browser, then the browser downloads the result to your device.

What it does

  • Offers PNG, JPG, WebP, and AVIF when the browser can encode them.
  • Uses your saved quality and filename preferences.
  • Keeps a small local history that you can clear at any time.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload images to a conversion service.
  • It does not inject affiliate links, ads, or analytics.
  • It does not scan every image on a page in the background.

Honest limits

For images on X's pbs.twimg.com CDN, Chrome asks for optional access after you select an image. If allowed, the extension fetches only that image without cookies or a page referrer, then converts it locally. Other protected sites may block conversion. Animated GIFs are exported as a still first frame, AVIF depends on the browser's encoder, and very large images are refused to limit memory use.

Permissions, in plain English

Active tab + scripting
Read the selected page for conversion and show feedback only after your right-click action.
Context menus
Add the Safe Image Save submenu to image right-click menus.
Downloads
Send the converted file to the browser's normal download flow.
Storage
Keep your preferences and optional recent-save list on this device.
Alarms
Clear the small success or error badge after it has been shown.
X image CDN (optional)
After you choose an X image, read that image from pbs.twimg.com without cookies or a page referrer so conversion stays local.
